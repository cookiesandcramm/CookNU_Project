About Us

What is Cook'N U?

Cook'N U is a vibrant, community-driven recipe platform built for food lovers of all skill levels. Whether you're a seasoned home chef or just starting to experiment in the kitchen, Cook'N U is your go-to space to discover, share, and celebrate food.

Our Mission

We believe that great food brings people together. Our mission is to make the joy of cooking accessible to everyone — by creating a welcoming space where real recipes from real people are shared freely, without the fluff.

What You Can Do Here

  • Browse community-submitted recipes across every category
  • Share your own creations and get hearts from fellow food lovers
  • Save your favorite recipes and build your personal cookbook
  • Connect with a growing community that loves food as much as you do

Our Story

Cook'N U was born out of a shared love for good food and the frustration of not having a single, focused place where everyday cooks could shine. We set out to build something simple, beautiful, and truly community-first.

From sinigang to soufflé, from late-night snacks to weekend feasts — every recipe on this platform has a person behind it, and we're proud to give them a stage.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you post a recipe, we store your recipe content, photos, and cooking details. We also collect general usage data to improve the platform.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your Cook'N U account
  • To display your recipes and profile to other community members
  • To send you verification codes and important account notifications
  • To improve and personalize your experience on the platform

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which employs industry-standard encryption and security practices.

Sharing Your Data

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your email address is only used for account authentication and will never be displayed publicly.

Your Rights

You may delete your recipes or account at any time. If you have any questions about your data, please reach out to us through our Contact page.

Cookies

Cook'N U uses minimal browser storage only to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

Terms of Service

Effective: January 2025

1. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account on Cook'N U, you agree to these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

2. User Accounts

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account. You must provide a valid email address and must not impersonate another person.

3. Content Guidelines

  • Only post recipes and food-related content
  • Do not post content that is offensive, harmful, or misleading
  • Ensure any photos you upload are yours or that you have rights to use them
  • Do not plagiarize recipes — give credit where it's due

4. Community Standards

Cook'N U is a respectful, inclusive community. Harassment, hate speech, or disruptive behavior will result in immediate account suspension.

6. Termination

We reserve the right to suspend or delete accounts that violate these terms. You may also delete your own account at any time from your profile settings.
